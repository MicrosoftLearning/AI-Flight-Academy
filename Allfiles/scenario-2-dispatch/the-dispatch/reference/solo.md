# The Single Triager

## The "before" - one person routing the queue

> **Don't edit this file.** It's the comparison point. The room the participant builds is measured against it, so if this moves, the comparison stops meaning anything.

You're one person working the intake queue. Each request comes in, you pick the one team that seems the best fit, and you send it there. You're deciding *where it goes*, not *what the plan is*, because that's all one triager can hold at once. That blind spot is the reason the room exists.

---

## What one triager produces

One owner per request. A clean hand-off. No plan, no reuse, no repointing - just "this looks like a Content & Insights thing, send it over."

It's not wrong, exactly. It's *thin*. It can't say "the real audience is partners, not customers," and it can't say "build this once and reuse it live and regionally," because holding all of that at once is what a *room* is for.

---

## The decisions - already done, handed over with the pack

One pass over the sample requests. This is what a room has to beat. Notice what it can't do: it gives *one owner per request*, and stops there.

| Request | Lone triager sends it to | The tell |
|---|---|---|
| RQ-01 · governance before GA | Content & Insights | **The tell.** Routes to the obvious owner and stops. Can't see that partners need it *first*, or that the path should be built once and reused by DPO (live) and Field & Partner (regional). One owner hides a three-team plan. |
| RQ-02 · partners misconfigure DLP | "the training team" | Picks a generic owner. Misses that the audience is explicitly partners and the fix is a hands-on lab the field can reuse. |
| RQ-03 · all employees, agentic AI, 30 min | Content & Insights | Defaults to content. Misses that it's a reach/first-touch play, not a durable module - and that no team cleanly owns "all employees." |
| RQ-05 · Copilot Studio cert | Credentials | Says "build a cert." Misses that the product churns (not cert-ready yet) and someone has to *prep* builders, which is a different job from *owning* the cert. |
| RQ-07 · field agent-deploy lab, EOQ | "the labs team" | Assigns the lab. Misses the build-time-vs-EOQ squeeze and that the audience is field, who'd reuse it live with customers. |

> **The point this makes:** every request got exactly one owner and no plan. The triager isn't *wrong* - it's *thin*. It can't repoint an audience or say "build once, reuse across teams," because it only holds one team at a time.

---

## What one triager can't see

It decides *where a request goes*. It can't decide *what to make, for whom, and who reuses it*, because it holds one owner at a time - never the whole room. Seat the teams, dispatch the same request to all of them at once, and where their positions split - the plan and the reuse - is everything the single triager missed.
