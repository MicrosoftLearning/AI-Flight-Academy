# Facilitator notes - the expected split

**Coach-only.** Don't hand this to participants; discovering the disagreement is the exercise. Use it to tell whether a room is genuinely splitting or just nodding along. A healthy Dispatch run produces **at least three distinct positions** on each formed request.

---

## RQ-01 · Agent governance before GA
- **DPO:** live readiness workshop for sellers/customers - but capacity is tight pre-GA.
- **Content & Insights:** evergreen Learning Path + an Applied Skills credential - *but* flags "a few settings may still shift" against certifiable objectives.
- **Product (ASN):** don't build a one-off - route it into ASN as a Learning-Agent journey.
- **Field & Partner:** partners *deploy* governance - repoint the audience to partners first.
- **MTTs:** split it - deep CAIP IT-pro track vs light M365 end-user track.
- **Likely dispatch:** owner C&I (+ DPO pilot) · audience partners-then-customers · shape LP + credential (phased) · disposition **split/reshape** · next-action open WI, loop Field & Partner.

## RQ-02 · Partners misconfiguring Purview DLP
- **Field & Partner:** yes - this is *our* audience (partners), landing regionally.
- **MTTs:** hands-on IT-pro lab/clinic - this is a doing problem.
- **Content & Insights:** a lab + troubleshooting guide, evergreen.
- **Product:** worth a guided sandbox in the platform?
- **DPO:** a fast live clinic while the lab gets built.
- **Split axis:** audience is settled (partners); the fight is **shape** (fast clinic vs built lab) and **speed vs durability**.

## RQ-03 · All employees "get" agentic AI in 30 min
- **Audience & Outbound:** short-form / social / internal campaign - reach play.
- **Content & Insights:** one tight module - but "not a certification" caps their instinct.
- **Product:** internal ASN moment?
- **DPO:** exec/leader-led sessions.
- **MTTs:** *not our audience* - passes.
- **Split axis:** **shape** (30-min short-form vs module) and **who owns internal** (no team is cleanly pointed at all-employees).

## RQ-05 · Copilot Studio builder certification
- **Content & Insights (Credentials):** yes in principle (Applied Skills/exam) - *but* "product changes often" fights certifiable stability. This is the tension.
- **Product:** it belongs in the ASN credentials ecosystem.
- **MTTs:** we'd train builders to *prep* for it (different job from owning it).
- **DPO:** deliver the prep programs.
- **Split axis:** **is it cert-ready?** (durability) and **own-the-cert vs prep-for-it**.

## RQ-07 · Field hands-on agent-deploy lab, EOQ
- **MTTs:** IT-pro hands-on lab - yes, our wheelhouse.
- **Content & Insights (Labs):** we build labs - but real-tenant labs are build-heavy with a maintenance tail; EOQ is tight.
- **Field & Partner:** field is the audience - yes, land it regionally.
- **DPO:** deliver it live in readiness sessions.
- **Product:** put the lab in the platform so it's reusable.
- **Split axis:** **shape** (lab vs live session) and **build-time/ownership** vs the EOQ deadline.

## The two ideas - expected behavior
- **IDEA-01 / IDEA-02:** the completeness check should flag missing fields (outcome/deadline/defined topic). The room's job is to **sharpen** - pin audience and outcome - and a legitimate disposition is **defer** or **decline-and-redirect** ("go back to the requester for X"). If a room confidently "routes" a rough idea without sharpening it, that's the miss to catch - a confident answer with nothing behind it.
