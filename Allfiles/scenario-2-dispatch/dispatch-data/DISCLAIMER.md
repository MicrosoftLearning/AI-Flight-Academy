# Disclaimer — read this before you use the team cards

The teams in `teams/` map to the **real Global Skilling organization** (post-FY27 reorg), but the cards name **no individuals** — they describe team charters, not people. **Everything about how each team "argues" — what makes it say yes, what it pushes back on, its voice — is a simplified teaching characterization, not any team's official position.** Real teams are more nuanced, collaborate constantly, and don't fit on a card. The cards exist to create productive disagreement for a two-hour exercise, nothing more.

The **requests** in `requests/` are fictional. Names, deadlines, and details are invented to make the room split in instructive ways. Don't treat any routing decision this exercise produces as a real skilling plan.

**Keep it internal.** These cards describe the internal Global Skilling org structure. Keep them on your own screen — don't paste them into a shared doc, a demo, or a public screenshot.

If a card no longer matches reality (the org moves fast), fix it — the cards are yours to edit. Grounding the room in your *actual* current teams is exactly the point.
