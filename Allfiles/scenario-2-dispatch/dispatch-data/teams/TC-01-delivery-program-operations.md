# TEAM CARD · TC-01
## 🎬 Delivery & Program Operations (DPO)

> **In one line:** The delivery engine. Turns skilling into *live, delivered* programs — and worries about who's teaching it and when.

---

## The team

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they own** | The operational delivery engine — the merged Commercial + Sales Skilling orgs, plus onboarding, global scheduling, program operations, and delivery enablement |
| **Measured on** | Programs delivered · seats filled · adoption/readiness outcomes · delivery cost |
| **Reports into** | Global Skilling |

## Who they serve

Customer orgs, partners, MCAPS sellers, Microsoft employees, and execs — anyone who needs to be *brought up to speed*, live.

## What they produce

Instructor-led training (virtual + in-person), premium customer engagements, Copilot adoption programs, onboarding journeys, readiness programs.

## Capacity & appetite

- **Bandwidth is the constraint.** Delivery is people-hours; the calendar is finite and booked out.
- **Motion-driven.** A launch, a GA date, a sales motion, or an onboarding wave is what makes something real for them.

## Says YES when
- There's a live audience that needs enablement *on a date* — a launch, a motion, an onboarding wave.
- The material is teachable **now** (it exists, or they can shape it fast).
- Someone will actually show up in a room (virtual or not).

## Says NO / routes elsewhere when
- The real need is evergreen self-serve that scales without a human in the room.
- There's no content to deliver yet — "there's nothing to teach until someone builds it."
- No motion, no date, no audience assembled → "come back when it's real."

## Format bias
Pushes work toward **a live thing**: a workshop, a clinic, an adoption program, an onboarding module in an instructor's hands.

## In the room
- **Opening move:** "Who's the audience, and when do they need to be ready?"
- **Pushes for:** a date, a delivery owner, a motion to hang it on.
- **Resists:** open-ended asks with no audience or deadline; "build it and they will come."
- **Their tell:** talks in calendars and capacity, not topics.

## What a good routing decision gives them
A named audience, a date, and a clear "yes this is deliverable / no this needs building first" — so they don't commit instructors to something that isn't ready.
