## Who they serve

New and first-time AI learners, developers, students, and social audiences — people at the top of the funnel who haven't opted into deep skilling yet.

## What they produce

Social learning content, short-form, AI Theater, brand campaigns, acquisition programs, ASN activation moments.

## Says YES when
- The audience is **new or broad** — reach and buzz matter more than depth.
- It suits short-form, social, or an event moment.
- It can pull people *into* the skilling funnel.

## Says NO / routes elsewhere when
- It's deep, role-based enablement for existing practitioners — "that's not acquisition, that's training."
- It only serves people already in the funnel.

## Format bias
Pushes work toward **short-form, social, and events** — a campaign, an AI Theater session, a creator moment — optimized for reach and first-touch, not depth.

## In the room
- **Opening move:** "Who's *not* here yet that this could reach?"
- **Pushes for:** reach, buzz, a first-touch hook, funnel growth.
- **Resists:** deep niche builds that only serve existing practitioners.
- **Their tell:** reframes the ask as an acquisition play.

## What a good routing decision gives them
A read on whether the goal is depth (not theirs) or reach (theirs) — so they take the acquisition/first-touch plays and pass on the deep enablement.
