# TEAM CARD · TC-03
## 🧑‍🏫 Technical Training / MTTs

> **In one line:** Live, hands-on *technical* delivery. First question is always "which audience - IT pro or end user?"

---

## The team

| | |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Regional delivery leads across Americas, EMEA, and Asia |
| **What they own** | Instructor-led technical delivery, realigned by audience track: **M365 Copilot End-User** vs **Cloud & AI Program (CAIP) IT Pro** |
| **Measured on** | Sessions delivered · technical depth/accuracy · learner capability gain · regional coverage |
| **Reports into** | Delivery & Program Operations (DPO) |

## Who they serve

IT pros, developers, and end users - split hard down the middle by how technical the audience is.

## What they produce

Live instructor-led technical training - deep configuration/admin sessions for IT pros, lighter capability sessions for end users.

## Capacity & appetite

- **Audience track decides everything.** The same topic becomes two different deliverables depending on who's in the seat.
- **Depth is their bar.** For IT pros, a shallow session is worse than none - it burns credibility.

## Says YES when
- There's genuine **technical depth** to teach, hands-on.
- The audience track is clear (or they can split it into IT-pro vs end-user).
- It rewards a live, guided, do-it-with-me format.

## Says NO / routes elsewhere when
- It's non-technical or awareness-level - "that's not a training, that's a comms."
- No clear audience track - "who is this for, IT or end users? It can't be both in one room."
- It's better as self-serve content than a live session.

## Format bias
Pushes work toward **a live technical session**, and almost always toward **splitting by track**: a deep CAIP IT-pro lab *and* a lighter M365 end-user session.

## In the room
- **Opening move:** "IT pro or end user? Because that's two different builds."
- **Pushes for:** technical accuracy, hands-on depth, the right audience track.
- **Resists:** one-size-fits-all sessions; awareness fluff dressed up as training.
- **Their tell:** immediately splits the audience before talking about content.

## What a good routing decision gives them
A named audience track (IT pro vs end user) and confirmation there's real technical depth - so they don't get handed an awareness ask that isn't theirs, or a single session that should've been two.
