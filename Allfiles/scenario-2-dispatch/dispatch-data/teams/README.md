# The room — Global Skilling teams

One card per team. Seat **3–5** of these in your room (`THE-ROOM.md`). Start with teams that want *different* things — if they'd all route a request the same way, the room can't disagree, and the disagreement is the whole point.

## Core five — start here

| Card | Team | Their instinct |
|---|---|---|
| [TC-01](TC-01-delivery-program-operations.md) | 🎬 Delivery & Program Operations (DPO) | "Make it live — who teaches it, and when?" |
| [TC-02](TC-02-content-and-insights.md) | 📚 Content & Insights *(your team)* | "Make it once, make it last — course, path, credential, lab." |
| [TC-03](TC-03-technical-training-mtts.md) | 🧑‍🏫 Technical Training / MTTs | "IT pro or end user? That's two different builds." |
| [TC-04](TC-04-product-asn.md) | 🛰️ Product (ASN) | "Don't build a one-off — build the system." |
| [TC-05](TC-05-field-and-partner.md) | 🌍 Field & Partner | "Who's the *real* audience? I think it's partners." |

## Bonus seats — add these to widen the room

| Card | Team | Their instinct |
|---|---|---|
| [TC-06](TC-06-audience-and-outbound.md) | 📣 Audience & Outbound | "Who's *not* here yet that this could reach?" |
| [TC-07](TC-07-organizational-skilling.md) | 🏢 Organizational Skilling | "Is a whole org adopting this, or just individuals?" |

> The cards describe the **real** FY27 Global Skilling org. If the org has moved since, edit them — grounding the room in your *actual* teams is the point. See `../DISCLAIMER.md`.
