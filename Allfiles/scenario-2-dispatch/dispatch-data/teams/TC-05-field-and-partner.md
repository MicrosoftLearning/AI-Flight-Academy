# TEAM CARD · TC-05
## 🌍 Field & Partner

> **In one line:** Always asks the question the room forgot — *who's the real audience, and who lands it in market?*

---

## The team

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they own** | Landing and scaling skilling programs into the field & partner ecosystem — regional execution, partner activation, reporting |
| **Measured on** | Field/partner reach · regional adoption · partner activation · in-market execution |
| **Reports into** | Global Skilling |

## Who they serve

MCAPS sellers, field orgs, partners, and regional leads — the people who take skilling to customers at scale.

## What they produce

Regional skilling plans, field & partner readiness, adoption campaigns, in-market execution.

## Capacity & appetite

- **Reach through others.** They rarely deliver to end customers directly — they enable the field and partners who do.
- **Region matters.** "Global" usually means "someone has to land this in EMEA, Asia, and the Americas differently."

## Says YES when
- The **real** audience is partners, sellers, or the field — even if the request said "customers."
- It needs regional landing and scaling, not a single central drop.
- Partners can multiply the reach.

## Says NO / routes elsewhere when
- It's a direct-to-customer or internal-only need with no field/partner motion.
- It's a content build with no in-market execution attached.

## Format bias
Pushes work toward **repointing the audience** (often to partners/sellers first) and toward **regionalized rollout** — "who lands this in each region?"

## In the room
- **Opening move:** "Wait — who's the *real* audience here? Because I think it's partners."
- **Pushes for:** the right audience, a landing/activation plan, regional coverage.
- **Resists:** "customers" as a lazy default; central drops with no one to land them.
- **Their tell:** re-points the audience before anyone talks about shape.

## What a good routing decision gives them
An honest answer to "who is this really for?" and whether there's a field/partner motion — so they aren't handed a central content drop with no path into market, and the audience isn't defaulted to "customers" when partners are the actual lever.
