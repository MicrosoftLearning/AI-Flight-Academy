# What we are looking for

People who show up for individuals.

A champion here is someone who helps one person at a time, in small rooms, when
nobody is watching. The mentoring nobody logs. The question answered at 9pm.

Weigh depth of peer support above reach. Someone whose colleagues describe them
as the person they go to when stuck matters more than someone with a widely
downloaded template. Reach is not the same as care, and a large audience is not
evidence of either.
