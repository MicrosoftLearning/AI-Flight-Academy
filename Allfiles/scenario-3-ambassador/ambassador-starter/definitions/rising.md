# What we are looking for

People on the way up, not people who have already arrived.

A champion here is someone whose recent months look better than their early
ones. Rising peer support, work starting to get reused, showing up in places
they did not used to.

Weigh trajectory above current standing. Someone strong and flat matters less
than someone weaker and climbing. Discount anyone already recognized more than
once: the cohort exists to reach people the program has not reached yet.
