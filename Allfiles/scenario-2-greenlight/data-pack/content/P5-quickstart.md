# Quickstart: Set up your Copilot workspace

Follow these steps to provision your Copilot workspace and begin summarizing conversations. Estimated time: 10 minutes.

## Step 1 — Open the Copilot Workspace Provisioning Center

From your desktop, open the Copilot Workspace Provisioning Center and select **Create workspace**. Your workspace is where your summarization history and saved prompts are kept.

## Step 2 — Assign your summarization scope

In the workspace settings, choose which conversations Copilot may summarize. Most users select all channels and chats across the organization.

## Step 3 — Run your first summary

Select any conversation and choose **Summarize**. Review the output.

## Step 4 — Configure retention

Set how long your summaries are kept. The default is indefinite. Change this to match your local requirements.

## Step 5 — Enable cross-store visibility

To compare performance across locations, enable cross-store visibility. This lets you summarize conversations from any store in your district, which is useful for benchmarking.

## Step 6 — Verify your admin role

Before completing setup, confirm that you have been assigned the Workspace Administrator role. Provisioning cannot be completed without it. If you do not have this role, request it before continuing.

## Step 7 — Install the summarization add-in

Download and install the Copilot Summarization add-in on your workstation. Accept the elevation prompt when it appears.

## Step 8 — Validate

Return to Step 3 and re-run your summary. It should now include cross-store data and be retained according to the policy you set in Step 4.

## Troubleshooting

**Provisioning Center will not open.** Confirm your workspace has been created. If the option is greyed out, your account may not be licensed.

**Cannot enable cross-store visibility.** This requires district-level permissions. Contact your IT admin.

**Add-in will not install.** You will need local administrator rights on the machine. Contact your IT admin to have the install performed for you.

**Summaries are empty.** Ensure your summarization scope was set correctly in Step 2, and that the conversation contains at least one message.

## Next steps

Once your workspace is provisioned, explore saved prompts and scheduled summaries. Contact your IT admin if you need additional permissions.
