# Turn your shift chat into a handover note

Your shift chat already has everything the next shift needs. It is just spread across ninety messages. Copilot can pull it into one short note in about a minute, so you are not retyping it at the end of a long day.

## Before you start

Nothing to install. Nothing to turn on. If you can open Teams on your phone, you are ready.

You will need the chat your team used during the shift. Any chat you are already part of will work.

## Do this

**1. Open the shift chat in Teams.**

**2. Tap the Copilot button at the top of the chat.**

**3. Ask for what you want.** Type it the way you would say it. For example:

> *Summarize today's shift chat for the next shift lead. Include anything unfinished, anything that needs a decision, and anything about safety.*

**4. Read what comes back.** Change anything that is wrong or missing. It is your note, not Copilot's.

**5. Paste it into the chat for the next shift.**

## What it looks like when it worked

You get a short summary with a few bullet points. It should name the things your team actually talked about — a late delivery, a register that kept locking, a call-out for tomorrow.

If the note reads like it could have been written about any store on any day, it did not work. Ask again and say what you want it to cover.

## When it does not work

**Copilot says it cannot find the conversation.** You are probably in a different chat than you think. Go back to the chat itself, then tap Copilot from inside it.

**The summary misses something important.** Ask a second time and name the thing. *"What did we say about the freezer?"* works better than asking for a better summary.

**The button is not there.** Some chats are too new to summarize. Wait until there are a few messages in it.

If none of that helps, call the Retail Ops Support line. You do not need to raise a ticket first.

## What to check before you send it

Read it once. Copilot summarizes what was said — including things that were wrong when someone said them. If the chat contained a bad number, the summary will contain the same bad number.

You are still the one signing off on the handover.

## Show a supervisor

This takes about two minutes to teach. Stand next to one of your shift supervisors, have them do it on their own phone, and let them ask their own chat. They will remember it better than if they watch you do it.

Once all four supervisors can do it, you are out of the handover business.
