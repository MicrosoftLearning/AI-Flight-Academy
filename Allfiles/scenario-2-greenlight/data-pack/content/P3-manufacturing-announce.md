# Copilot summarization is now available across Contoso Industries

Meeting and chat summarization in Microsoft Teams is now switched on for your business unit. There is no sign-up and no waitlist. If you have Teams, you have it.

Teams at three sites have been using it for the last two months. The feedback has been consistent: it saves the most time on the parts of the week people like least.

## What it does

Copilot can take a meeting you attended, or a chat you are part of, and give you back a short summary with the decisions and the follow-ups pulled out. You can ask it for whatever you need:

> *What did we decide about the schedule?*

> *List the action items from this conversation and who owns each one.*

> *Summarize this for someone who was not here.*

It works whether or not you were present, as long as you already had access to the conversation.

## Where teams got the most out of it

**Catching up on something you missed.** Ask Copilot where a conversation landed instead of reading all of it.

**Long threads.** That forty-message chat where a decision got made somewhere in the middle — ask what was decided and who owns it.

**Passing work on.** Ask for a summary written for the person picking it up next, and paste it where they will see it.

## Getting started

Open a conversation from this week. Select Copilot. Ask it a question you already know the answer to.

Comparing what it says against what you remember is the quickest way to learn when to rely on it.

## A note on accuracy

Copilot summarizes what was said. If something was said incorrectly, it will appear incorrectly in the summary. Read it before you pass it on.

We will share what teams are doing with it over the coming weeks. If you find something that works well, tell us and we will pass it on.
