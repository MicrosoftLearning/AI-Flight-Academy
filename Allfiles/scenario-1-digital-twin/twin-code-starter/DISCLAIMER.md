## Why it ships filled in

So the starter answers on the first command, and so nobody spends hack time writing a
persona or putting their own work into a shared exercise. Everything you build during the
session runs against Jordan.

## Using it for yourself

Two ways, whenever you want:

- **Run `python onboard.py`** - a short interview that writes your own `persona.md`,
  `voice.md`, and `standards.md` from your answers, and backs Jordan up as `*.jordan.md`.
- **Edit the files by hand** - they're plain markdown in the Agent Skills format. A Copilot
  surface with access to your work can draft them from your sent mail and calendar, and you
  correct what it got wrong.

Keep whatever you write local. Nothing personal belongs in a shared repo.
