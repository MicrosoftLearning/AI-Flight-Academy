## Question

What did I promise that I have not delivered?

## Pull

Two sources, both required:

- **Mail** – my sent items, last 21 days. For anything that reads like a commitment, pull the rest
  of that thread and check my later sent messages for the delivery.
- **Teams** – my recent chats, most recently active first, and the messages I wrote in any chat
  active inside the window. **Most small promises are made here, not in mail.**

Skip meeting responses, comment notifications and automated mail – they are never commitments, so
they do not count toward `checked`.

Report both counts in `sources`.

## Decide

An item qualifies when **I said I would do something** and nothing since shows I did it.

A commitment is a first-person future statement with an object: "I'll send the numbers", "I'll get
you a date Friday", "leave it with me". Hedges are not commitments – "I might", "we could probably",
"happy to take a look at some point".

Exclude:

- anything I can see I delivered in a later message
- anything the other person has since said is no longer needed
- commitments made in the last 24 hours
- anything where the date I gave has not arrived yet – it is not late, it is pending

**Do not exclude something because it looks small.** A two-line reply I promised on Tuesday is
exactly what this panel is for.

Order by `age`, oldest first. Where a date was promised and has passed, put those above the rest.

## Item

- **label** – what I promised, in my words, under about ten words
- **meta** – who I promised, and the date. If I named a due date, show that instead
- **age** – whole days since I promised
- **url** – the thread

## Empty

Nothing outstanding in the last three weeks. This panel only sees promises made in writing – one
made out loud in a meeting will never show up here.
