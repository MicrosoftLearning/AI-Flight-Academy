## Question

What have I asked for that never came back?

## Pull

My sent mail and my Teams messages, last 21 days. For anything that looks like a request, pull the
rest of that thread so the replies can be checked.

## Decide

An item qualifies when **I asked someone for something specific** and nothing answering it has
arrived since.

A request is specific when it names a deliverable, a decision, a number, or a yes/no. "Let me know
what you think" qualifies. "Keep me posted" does not.

Exclude:

- anything where a reply arrived that addresses the ask, even partially
- anything I have already chased in the last 3 days – they know
- anything asked in the last 2 days, which is not late yet
- requests to a distribution list or channel with no named person on the hook
- anything I have since answered myself, or that the calendar shows was covered in a meeting

When two items involve the same person, keep both. Who is slowest is the useful pattern.

Order by `age`, oldest first. The oldest unanswered ask is almost always the one that matters.

## Item

- **label** – what I asked for, in my words, under about ten words
- **meta** – who I asked, and the date I asked
- **age** – whole days since I asked
- **url** – the thread

## Empty

Nothing outstanding. Every ask in the last three weeks came back.
